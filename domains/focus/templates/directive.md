# Directive title

Follow the directive contract for status, priority, and `parent` (optional:
another directive filename; remove the key when unused). Adopter workflows may
add their own frontmatter keys, such as a one-line presentation text; keep them
short and never duplicate the body.

## Purpose

State the direction and why it matters, who established it, and when.

## Scope

- Included: work this directive covers.
- Excluded: adjacent work it does not cover, and what it does not approve.

## Value scope

State the benefit pursued and how a contribution to it is recognized.

## Completion evidence

- Counts as progress or delivery: the evidence that proves it.
- Does not prove it: signals that look like completion but are not (for
  example a merge without a release).

## Decision points

- Desired result, next action or decision, linked thread, and open uncertainty.

## Attention evidence

- Raises attention: evidence that increases priority.
- Lowers attention: evidence that decreases priority, including handoff to
  downstream monitoring.

## Boundaries

- Holds: explicit user holds this directive respects, with thread links.
- Own action versus monitoring: where the user's action ends.
- Authorization: what this directive never authorizes (for example code changes
  or external writes); each external action is still presented for review.

Link source threads; do not copy their histories here.
