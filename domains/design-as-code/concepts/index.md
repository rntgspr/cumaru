# Concepts

Use this pillar for deliberate design alternatives. A concept records the
problem, evidence, audience, constraints, prototype links, trade-offs, and
selection criteria. Link a selected concept and its research to the delivery
plan. Keep it until `cumaru-absorb` validates the durable result and permits
cleanup; shared concepts remain while other active work still needs them.
Rejected directions follow the guarded rejection flow in `cumaru-concept`.
