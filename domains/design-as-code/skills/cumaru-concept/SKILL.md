# Cumaru concept

Use concepts for deliberate design directions, not generic brainstorming. Each
concept links its research evidence, audience, prototype or sketch, constraints,
trade-offs, and criteria for selection.

## Create

1. Confirm a kebab-case slug and create concepts/<slug>/ and its index.
2. Use the concept template. Set status to considering and cite research.
3. Describe the problem, alternatives, states, responsive implications,
   accessibility, and open questions.
4. Validate with cumaru tree concepts --rows and cumaru doctor.

## Select

1. Compare alternatives against user outcome, evidence, implementation cost,
   accessibility, and product constraints.
2. Do not promote until a tracker-backed intake brief exists.
3. Create the matching plan, transfer the selected direction and rejected
   alternatives, then remove the transient concept after its claims are retained.

## Reject

Confirm with the user, record the rationale in the related research or plan when
useful, then remove the concept directory.
