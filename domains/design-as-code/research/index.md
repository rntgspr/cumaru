# Research

Use this pillar for audience research, competitive reviews, usability studies,
accessibility audits, experiments, and evidence attached to active design work.
Create `research/<slug>/index.md` from `templates/research.md`, retaining
original evidence provenance and separating observations from interpretations.
Promote accepted findings into specs before removing consumed research through
`cumaru-absorb`. Keep studies still needed by other active work; link retained
source evidence from the durable finding when needed.
